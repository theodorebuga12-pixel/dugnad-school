Welcome to Dugnad

Dugnad is a contemporary, fast-growing kindergarten and daycare in Kavule-Jomayi Estate, along Matuga-Semuto Road in Wakiso District.

We are dedicated to an activity-based curriculum that aims at the whole child, through formal and informal early childhood learning programmes.

We believe every child is unique and should be helped to bring out their potential. Our talented and experienced staff work with a low teacher-pupil ratio, guiding children towards lifelong learning built on foundational skills and critical thinking.

How children learn here


Computer learning lab

Tablets, headsets and about 200 learning apps

Our lab uses tablets with headsets, pre-installed with about 200 educational apps covering logic, literacy and numeracy. Children also play hands-on games such as puzzles and spelling games, and work with learner cards, station cards, an app book, training manuals and videos.

The apps and games work together to give each child a complete learning arena. Many children crack the code of reading in just a few weeks. The lab is powered by Leap Learning Labs, Norway (www.leaplearning.no).

Library and e-learning

Our library and e-resources teach children the value of research and discovery. Through the e-resources, learners can interact with fellow learners and teachers from our partners in Europe and the US.

Kindergarten also offers online classes, at a cost, to children who prefer to study from home.

Clubs and sports

Clubs include IT, Scouts and Guides, Chess, Music, Dance and Drama (MDD), Cookery, and Christian and Muslim networks. Club membership is UGX 30,000 per child. Sports are free for all children. Swimming lessons with experts at a partner training centre are UGX 60,000 per term.

Concerts and tours

Children show their skills in art and design, technology, narration, dancing and communication at school concerts, and widen their knowledge on educational tours.

Meals

Three balanced meals support children’s physical and mental growth: break tea, lunch and evening tea. All children get breakfast. Lunch and evening tea are served to daycare and primary children.

How to join

Daycare takes children from 6 months, kindergarten from 3 years and primary from 6 years.

Bring these on admission

  • 2 passport-size photos of the child
  • 2 passport-size photos of the parents
  • A copy of the child’s birth certificate
  • The child’s immunization card

In some cases we test a child’s numeracy and English literacy to decide which class they join. Application forms are also available at the Head teacher’s office.

What we ask of parents

  • Support the school’s objectives and programmes whenever called upon.
  • Be a role model for learning, and help your child take charge of it.
  • Make sure your child attends school consistently.
  • Tell the school about your child’s behaviour and health status.
  • Encourage good discipline and remind your child of the school rules.
  • Keep TV to a minimum, and pay attention to what your child loves.
  • Follow up with the teachers to track your child’s learning.

Drop-off and pick-up

Every child must be brought and collected by a parent or caregiver registered with the school. If the person collecting changes, tell the administration who it will be and how to identify them.

We do not release children to unregistered persons. Children left beyond official working hours are looked after at the parent’s cost. Parents who collect late pay a fine of UGX 5,000 per child.

Fees

All amounts are in Ugandan shillings (UGX), as set out in the school brochure.

Club membership is UGX 30,000 per child. Sports are free. Transport rates depend on distance. Please confirm current amounts with the school office before you pay.
